Plant-friendly bedroom planner — coming soon

Make Room for Greenery That Works for You

A small bedroom can feel fresh and calming without filling every surface with pots. Choose plants suited to your room's light and give each one a practical place.

This upcoming small bedroom plant planner is being designed to help you organise indoor plants, use awkward corners thoughtfully and plan a renter-friendly bedroom refresh.

Tip: Check your room's light before choosing plants. Keep pots away from walkways and unstable shelves, and check plant safety if pets or children share your home.

What the Planner Will Help You Do

01. Plan Around Light

Assess the natural light in your bedroom before choosing plants for a windowsill, shelf or darker corner.

02. Make Space Wisely

Plan plant placement without blocking drawers, bedside access, windows or walking routes.

03. Refresh on a Budget

Plan a simple greenery update using shelves, pots and surfaces you already have where practical.
